In 2022, we had the chance to work with Duolingo on a small microsite for the upcoming football world cup in Qatar.

The idea was to create a simple and fun way to help users learn how to cheer on or insult teams in other languages, and that resulted in a playful and heavily animated site with duolingo's mascot Duo alternating between chherful and angry based on the user's choice.

Do you also want to learn some new cheers or insults in other languages? Visit the [website](https://fowllanguage.duolingo.com/), and have fun!
