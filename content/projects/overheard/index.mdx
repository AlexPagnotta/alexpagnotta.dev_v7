What is Overheard? Well, it's not that easy to explain. It's mostly a social media thing: they collect the funniest and weirdest conversations people _overhear_ on city streets and share them with their community. Some are about the latest trends, some probably should've stayed private.

</Aside>

The goal of the project was to create a new website that captured Overheard's fun and quirky brand, and that could help attract potential sponsors and future employees.
