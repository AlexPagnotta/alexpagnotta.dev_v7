I'm **Alex Pagnotta**, and this is my personal site. It's not really a blog or a portfolio, more a place where I share whatever I find interesting, work related or not. I'm not a big fan of social networks and I rarely use them, so think of this as my own little Twitter or Instagram.

I'm a principal developer at [WILD](https://www.wild.as). I work across the whole stack, frontend and backend, and over the years I've had the chance to build projects for clients like [Duolingo](/projects/duolingo), [Brawl Stars](/projects/brawl-stars), [IKEA](/projects/ikea) and [many others](/?filter=projects). These days a big part of my role is helping the company adopt AI, from the internal tools and workflows we use every day to the AI products we build for our clients.

Work aside, I like to keep myself busy with hobbies and side projects, especially [making things](/?filter=make). I think it's a great time to be a maker, since with 3D printers and AI helping with the software and the research, pretty much anyone can build what they have in mind, and I'm really enjoying it.
